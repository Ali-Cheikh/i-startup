What kind of projects does I-StartUp take on?

Web applications, MVPs, business software, workflow automation, dashboards, and internal systems for growing teams.

Can the team work with an existing product?

Yes. We can take ownership of a feature or system, improve an existing workflow, or work alongside your team.

Can I-StartUp manage domains and hosting?

Yes. We can help with domain and DNS management, hosting, SSL, email setup, deployment, and ongoing updates.

Where is I-StartUp based?
