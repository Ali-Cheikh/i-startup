# I-StartUp color systems

Use one color system at a time. The website uses the high-contrast Tech palette with white paper and black structure. The Business and Neutral palettes are separate directions for social and campaign content; do not combine them in the same interface.

## 1. Tech — website and product content

- Deep blue / primary: `#03045E`
- Black / structural text: `#090910`
- White / paper and cards: `#FFFFFF`
- Red / markers and status: `#E63946`
- Hot orange / primary actions: `#FF5400`
- Orange / secondary highlight: `#FF9E00`
- Grid and rules: `#B8B8C5`
- Muted copy: `#353545`

Use white text on deep blue and black. Keep body text near-black on white. Use orange for primary actions and red for labels, numbering, and small status marks. Avoid pale tinted panels; create separation with drafting lines, hard borders, and offset shadows.

## 2. Business — editorial content

- Deep teal: `#0B3C49`
- Deep sage: `#1A2F25`
- Muted terracotta: `#C87A53`
- Sand: `#EED9C4`
- Acid lime: `#CCFF00` (small graphic accents only)

Use this palette for business and study graphics, not website components.

## 3. Neutral — minimalist posts

- Slate: `#E2E8F0`
- Deep blue type: `#03045E`
- Desaturated photography and restrained shadows

Keep this direction for minimal social posts. It is not a light theme for the website.
