Our approach

Own the outcome, from plan to production.

Our work connects product engineering with day-to-day operations. We build applications and platforms around how people actually work, then support the details that keep those systems useful after launch.

The I-StartUp team has delivered work across learning technology, recruitment operations, warehouse data capture, digital identity, and business automation. We use short feedback loops: understand the problem, ship a useful version, learn from use, and improve it.

Our capabilities span frontend and backend development, APIs, databases, automation, domains, deployments, and business email. Clients can work directly with I-StartUp tech lead Ali Cheikh for clear technical ownership and decisions.
