Engagement Terms

How we start.

To ensure a smooth project kickoff and protect both parties, all engagements follow this standard process:

  • Discovery & Proposal: We define the exact scope, deliverables, and timeline.
  • Deposit: A 50% advance deposit is required to secure your project slot and initiate discovery/kickoff.
  • Milestones: The remaining balance is invoiced based on agreed-upon milestones or project completion.
  • Handover: Full source code, asset transfer, and documentation are provided only upon final payment.
  • Post-Launch: Maintenance begins after the standard 30-day warranty period.

What We Do Not Do

We focus on our strengths. To maintain high quality, we do not:

  • Compete on "cheap" template builds.
  • Provide ongoing hourly content entry (we build you a system to do it yourself).
  • Offer unverified legal, tax, or accounting compliance guarantees.
